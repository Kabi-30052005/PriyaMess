Our Story

The Taste of Chettinad, Unchanged

Sri Priya Mess is a celebrated Chettinad restaurant and non-vegetarian mess nestled in the cultural heartland of Karaikudi, Tamil Nadu. Every dish carries the legacy of authentic Chettinad cooking — rich spice blends, slow-cooked gravies, and bold flavours passed down through generations.

Located on A.R.A Street, just 0.93 km from Karaikkudi Railway Station, Sri Priya Mess has been welcoming hungry souls who seek real, unadulterated Chettinad non-vegetarian food. Our kitchen uses traditional techniques and fresh local ingredients to bring you the same taste that has earned us over 2,700 glowing reviews.
